Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

1. Overview & Zero Personal Data Collection

Denetflix is a rare and cult retro movie discovery application covering films from 1960 to 1999. We respect your privacy and operate without user accounts, registrations, or personal profiles.

We do not collect, ask for, or store your name, email address, phone number, billing details, or persistent user identifiers.

2. How Session & Quiz Information Is Used

When you use the Denetflix quiz, your selected preferences (decade, genres, production region, mood, viewing context, exclusions, and selected country code for regional availability) are processed ephemerally solely to rank matching recommendations and check regional availability during your active browser session.

Once you close or refresh the page, quiz selections are not retained on Denetflix servers as a personal history.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

Denetflix does not set advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels, or behavioral analytics profiles. Standard technical caching may occur in browser memory or server response caches to speed up movie poster, trailer, and availability lookups without storing personal data.

Third-party external websites you choose to visit (such as Amazon or streaming platforms) and embedded video players may set their own cookies in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

4. Amazon Associates Program & Affiliate Disclosure

Denetflix participates in affiliate marketing programs. As an Amazon Associate, Denetflix earns from qualifying purchases made through physical media search links on this site, at no extra cost to you.

  • For English and international regions, physical media links route to Amazon.com using Associate tracking ID jr0605-20.
  • When Italy (IT) is selected as your country, physical media links route to Amazon.it using Associate tracking ID 280177-21.

When you click an Amazon link, Amazon may place cookies or tracking parameters on your device to attribute eligible purchases under Amazon’s own Privacy Notice and Conditions of Use.

5. Third-Party Services & Data Sources

To deliver accurate movie metadata, posters, availability, and trailers, Denetflix may communicate with:

  • TMDB (The Movie Database) & JustWatch: Used when configured to retrieve verified movie metadata, poster artwork, and regional streaming/rental/purchase listings.
  • Wikipedia REST API: Used purely to retrieve archival poster thumbnails for curated catalog titles without sending personal user data.
  • YouTube (Privacy-Enhanced Mode): Trailers open only when you click the Trailer button, loading via youtube-nocookie.com subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Server-Side AI Copy Enhancement: When enabled, only anonymous movie metadata and non-identifying quiz filter keywords are sent server-side to generate playful match notes.

6. Children’s Privacy, Rights & Updates

Denetflix is a general-audience movie discovery tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region).

Because Denetflix stores no personal user records, there is no personal profile data to access, export, or delete—simply closing your browser clears your active quiz session. Under GDPR and comparable privacy laws, you may also control or clear third-party cookies anytime via your browser settings.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically as features or third-party integrations evolve. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.
