1. Data Controller & Overview

Denetflix (www.denetflix.me) is an independent retro cinema discovery web application covering films from 1960 to 1999, operated by Jacopo Coccia (www.jacopococcia.com).

Denetflix does not require user accounts, registrations, logins, or subscriptions, and does not directly ask for your name, email address, phone number, or payment details. However, as detailed in this Privacy & Cookie Policy, third-party analytics, UX measurement, conversion attribution, and advertising tags loaded via Google Tag Manager may process pseudonymous technical and usage data (such as IP address, browser/device characteristics, page views, interaction events, and cookie/advertising identifiers).

2. How Session & Quiz Information Is Used

When you complete the Denetflix quiz, your selected preferences (decade, genres, production region, mood, deal-breaker exclusions, and selected country code for regional availability) are processed ephemerally during your active browser session solely to filter and rank 1960s–1990s movie recommendations and verify regional streaming availability.

Denetflix does not maintain personal user profiles or persistent server-side watch histories linked to your identity.

3. Cookie Policy & Technologies Deployed via Google Tag Manager

Denetflix uses Google Tag Manager (container GTM-NFKZCVHZ), provided by Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC, as a tag orchestration service. Google Tag Manager itself does not set profiling cookies, but it loads and manages the following third-party measurement, usability, and marketing tools:

  • Google Tag / Google Analytics 4 (GA4) (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC): Measures website traffic, page views, session engagement, approximate location, and device/browser metrics using first-party analytics cookies and pseudonymous client identifiers so we can understand how visitors use Denetflix and improve the catalog experience. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft Corporation): Provides behavioral UX analytics—including aggregated heatmaps, scroll depth, click patterns, and masked session replays—to detect interface bugs, broken layouts, and usability friction. Clarity automatically masks sensitive input fields and operates under Microsoft’s privacy framework. (Microsoft Privacy Statement)
  • Google Conversion Linker (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC): Stores ad-click and referral metadata in first-party cookies on this domain to accurately measure campaign clicks and conversion attribution across pages when users arrive from advertising links. (Google Advertising Technologies)
  • Meta Pixel (Facebook / Instagram Pixel) (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited / Meta Platforms, Inc.): Tracks page views and interaction events to measure the performance of Meta ad campaigns, attribute conversions, optimize ad delivery, and build aggregated remarketing audiences on Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram). (Meta Privacy Policy)

4. Amazon Associates Program & Affiliate Disclosure

Denetflix participates in affiliate marketing programs. As an Amazon Associate, Denetflix earns from qualifying purchases made through physical media links on this site, at no extra cost to you.

  • For English and international regions, physical media links route to Amazon.com using Associate tracking ID jr0605-20.
  • When Italy (IT) is selected as your country, physical media links route to Amazon.it using Associate tracking ID 280177-21.

When you click an Amazon link, Amazon may place cookies or tracking parameters on your device to attribute eligible purchases under Amazon’s own Privacy Notice and Conditions of Use.

5. Other Third-Party Services & Movie Data Sources

To deliver accurate movie metadata, posters, regional availability, and trailers, Denetflix also communicates with:

  • TMDB (The Movie Database) & JustWatch: Used to retrieve verified movie metadata, archival artwork, and regional streaming, rental, and purchase listings.
  • Wikidata, Wikipedia REST API & Internet Archive: Used to retrieve public filmography records, archival poster thumbnails, and public-domain availability without sending personal account data.
  • YouTube (Privacy-Enhanced Mode): Trailers only load when you explicitly click the Trailer button, using youtube-nocookie.com embeds subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Server-Side AI Copy Enhancement: When enabled, only anonymous movie metadata and non-identifying quiz keywords are processed server-side to generate match notes.

6. Legal Basis (GDPR / ePrivacy) & International Data Transfers

  • Strictly necessary technical processing (serving the web application, quiz state, security rate-limiting) relies on our legitimate interest in providing a secure, functioning service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Google LLC, Microsoft Corporation, and Meta Platforms, Inc. may process technical and usage data in the United States or other jurisdictions outside the EEA. Such transfers rely on applicable safeguards including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (DPF) and/or European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

7. Your Privacy Rights, Opt-Out Tools & Children’s Privacy

Under the GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and comparable privacy laws, you have the right to request information about personal data processing, object to or restrict analytics/marketing tracking, withdraw consent, or lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

You can disable or opt out of third-party cookies and tags at any time using:

  • Browser Controls: Block or clear first-party and third-party cookies in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge settings (or enable Global Privacy Control / Do Not Track).
  • Google Analytics Opt-Out: Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on and Google My Ad Center.
  • Microsoft Privacy & Clarity Controls: Microsoft Advertising & Privacy Settings.
  • Meta Pixel & Ad Preferences: Meta Ad Preferences and Your Online Choices (EU).

Children’s Privacy: Denetflix is a general-audience movie discovery tool and is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).
